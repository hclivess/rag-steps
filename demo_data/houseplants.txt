Houseplant Care Notes

Watering: Most houseplants die from overwatering, not underwatering. Check the top 2-3 cm of soil with a finger; water only when it feels dry. Pots must have drainage holes.

Light: Pothos, snake plants and ZZ plants tolerate low light. Succulents and cacti need several hours of direct sun. Monstera and fiddle-leaf figs prefer bright indirect light near a window.

Humidity: Tropical plants such as calatheas and ferns like 50-60 % humidity. Group plants together or use a pebble tray; misting has only a brief effect.

Fertiliser: Feed every 2-4 weeks during spring and summer with a diluted balanced fertiliser, and stop in winter when growth slows.

Repotting: Repot when roots circle the pot or grow out of the drainage holes, usually every 1-2 years, moving up only one pot size.
