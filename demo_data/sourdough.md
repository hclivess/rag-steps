# Sourdough Starter and Bread

## Starting a starter

Mix 50 g whole-wheat flour with 50 g water and leave it loosely covered at room temperature. Each day discard half and feed with fresh flour and water. After 5–7 days it should double in size within 4–8 hours of feeding, smell pleasantly sour, and be ready to bake with.

## The float test

Drop a spoonful of starter into water. If it floats it is full of gas and at peak activity, which is the best moment to mix your dough.

## Bulk fermentation

After mixing flour, water, salt and starter, the dough ferments for 4–6 hours at around 24 °C. During the first two hours perform three or four sets of stretch-and-folds to build strength. The dough is ready when it has grown by roughly 50 % and shows bubbles on the surface.

## Baking

Bake in a preheated Dutch oven at 250 °C: 20 minutes with the lid on to trap steam, then 20–25 minutes with the lid off until the crust is deep brown. Let the loaf cool for at least an hour before slicing, otherwise the crumb will be gummy.
